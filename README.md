# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is a popular sci-fi horror series on Netflix. I love it because of its suspenseful story and nostalgic 80s vibe.  

## Main Characters
- **Eleven** – A girl with telekinetic powers  
- **Mike** – The brave and loyal friend  
- **Dustin** – The funny and clever one  
- **Will** – The boy who goes missing in the first season  

## Reasons I Like This Series
1. Amazing storytelling that keeps you on edge  
2. Strong friendship between the characters  
3. The combination of mystery, adventure, and horror  
4. Awesome soundtrack and retro style  

## Favorite Season
**Season 1** – It introduces the Upside Down and the main characters in a thrilling way.

> "Friends don't lie." – Eleven
